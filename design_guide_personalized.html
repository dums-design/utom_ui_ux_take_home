🎨 Design Recommendations

How to use your personalized system

1. Primary Blue Usage

Use Steel Blue (#2B7A9B) for:

  • Primary buttons and CTAs
  • Navigation highlights
  • Links and interactive elements
  • Gradients with Sunset Orange

2. Accent Colors

Strategic accent usage:

  • Magenta: Featured content, premium badges
  • Vibrant Teal: Success states, positive actions
  • Sunset Orange: Energy, excitement, CTAs

3. Font Pairing Tips

Modern typography guidance:

  • Sora: Headings, titles, hero text
  • Plus Jakarta Sans: Body, UI, cards
  • Maintain strong hierarchy with size/weight
  • Geometric feel creates modern aesthetic
